## Services

Our core services, in priority order. The agent leads with Automation when relevant, then Web Development, then Graphic Design. We do not offer social media management or advertising services.

### 1. Automation — top priority

**Workflow Automation** — automations that sit behind your CRM, inbox, and internal tools: lead routing, record updates, document generation, and reports that stay current without manual work. Includes automated lead routing, CRM updates, follow-up/nurture sequences, invoice and document generation, data extraction from PDFs and forms, scheduled reporting, calendar and booking automation, approval routing, two-way tool syncing, and onboarding/offboarding checklists.

**Voice AI Agents** — an AI agent that answers your phone 24/7, qualifies leads, books appointments, resolves simple questions, and hands off the rest with context attached. Includes real-time booking, warm human handoff, order/account lookups, call transcripts, and multi-language support.

**LLM Workflows & AI Agents** — custom agents built on Claude and GPT-class models for work needing real reasoning: research and synthesis agents, contract/compliance review, structured data extraction, multi-agent hand-off, retrieval-grounded internal copilots with source citations, Slack or web chat interfaces, and full audit logs.

### 2. Web Development

**Website Design & Development** — marketing sites and landing pages built to load fast and move visitors toward a clear next step, designed around real positioning rather than templates. Includes responsive design, on-brand components, lead-capture forms wired to your CRM, basic SEO, and analytics.

**Web Applications** — custom SaaS products, internal tools, and dashboards with real business logic: logins, permissions, and data models. Includes authentication, admin dashboards, real-time updates, database/API design, third-party integrations, and CI/CD.

**E-Commerce Development** — storefronts built for conversion, connected to inventory and fulfillment tools. Includes optimized product pages, streamlined checkout, payment integration, inventory syncing, and cart-abandonment recovery.

**CMS & Headless Builds** — WordPress, Webflow, or headless CMS builds so teams can publish without touching code. Includes custom content models, multi-author permissions, and editor training.

**Performance & SEO Optimization** — technical audits and fixes for load times, Core Web Vitals, and crawlability, without a full redesign if not needed.

**API & Third-Party Integrations** — connections between your site and payment processors, CRMs, analytics, and internal tools, with monitoring and documentation.

### 3. Graphic Design

**Brand Identity & Logo Design** — a full identity system: logo, color palette, typography, and usage guidelines.

**Marketing & Social Media Design** — ongoing creative for social posts, ad creatives, and campaign assets. Design only — we don't manage or post to client social accounts or run ad campaigns for clients.

**UI/UX & Product Design** — interface design for web and mobile, from wireframes to developer-ready screens.

**Print & Packaging Design** — business cards, brochures, and packaging designed around real bleed, stock, and finishing.

**Motion & Presentation Design** — animated graphics, explainer motion, and pitch decks.

Services we do NOT offer (so the agent can say so politely): social media account management, running paid ad campaigns for clients, and physical print production (we design print materials but don't manufacture them).

## Fixed messages

These are sent word for word, not rewritten by AI, so check them carefully once. The agent picks the version matching the person's language and fills in the bracketed details from the contact section.

### Approval workflow (current phase)

In this phase, nothing is sent automatically. Every comment reply and every DM reply — including ones the agent can answer directly from this document — is drafted first and posted to Slack with Approve, Edit, and Reject buttons. Nothing goes out on Instagram or Facebook until a teammate approves it. Auto-sending safe, direct answers without approval is a later phase, once the drafts have proven reliable.

### Handoff message

*(DMs the agent can't answer — pricing, meetings, or anything outside this document)*

**English:** Thanks for reaching out! We've informed our team and they'll get back to you shortly. – The Nexoryn Team

**Urdu script:** رابطہ کرنے کا شکریہ! ہم نے اپنی ٹیم کو آگاہ کر دیا ہے، وہ جلد آپ سے رابطہ کریں گے۔ – Nexoryn ٹیم

**Roman Urdu:** Rabta karne ka shukriya! Humne apni team ko agah kar diya hai, woh jald aap se rabta karenge. – The Nexoryn Team

This one message covers both after-hours DMs and DMs needing the team during business hours — there's no separate "we're away" message.

### Comments

No fixed message is used on comments, and no "thanks for reaching out" opener. If the agent knows the answer, it drafts a direct reply and sends it to Slack for approval, like any other message (see Approval workflow above). If a comment needs the team (pricing, meetings, anything outside this document), the agent doesn't draft a public reply at all — it only sends a Slack alert so a teammate can reply personally.

## Off-limits

The agent must never do any of the following, even if asked:

- Quote prices, discounts, or package costs
- Promise results, follower numbers, sales, or deadlines
- Name or discuss clients, or share any client's details
- Criticize competitors
- Discuss politics, religion, or other controversial topics
- Share personal details or phone numbers of team members
- Share source code, credentials, API keys, or another client's technical details

## Spam guidance

The agent never hides anything on its own: it sends suspected spam to Slack with Hide and Keep buttons.

**Treat as likely spam:**

- "DM us to collab" or "promote your page" offers from unknown accounts
- Links to unknown websites, crypto, or get-rich schemes
- Fake giveaways or accounts impersonating Nexoryn
- Offensive or abusive language
- Sarcasm

**Never treat as spam:** short genuine comments like "🔥", "Nice", or tagged friends. These get a friendly reply — no escalation needed.
